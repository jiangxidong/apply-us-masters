## 学历门槛

- `✓ https://www.khoury.northeastern.edu/apply/masters-apply/` GPA 给三条并列尺子，不是单一量表：`"3.0 on a 4.0 scale / 8.0 on a 10.0 scale / 80 on a 100 scale"`
- `✓ https://www.khoury.northeastern.edu/apply/masters-apply/` 语言线是**建议线**、不是硬性最低线：`"Recommended TOEFL = 90≥ or IELTS 6.5≥"`
- `✓ https://www.khoury.northeastern.edu/apply/masters-apply/` **不 super-score**：`"Note: We will not super-score the IELTS or TOEFL."`
- 🔴 `✓ https://www.khoury.northeastern.edu/apply/masters-apply/` **不接受 Duolingo**：`"Duolingo test scores are not accepted."`
  → **接受的考试类型是学院级属性，不是学校级属性**：对照同校 Northeastern **College of Engineering (CoE)**（另一学院、另一 channel，不进本池）`✓ https://coe.northeastern.edu/academics-experiential-learning/graduate-school-of-engineering/graduate-admissions/` `"TOEFL: 79 overall (or 4.0 on the iBT 2026 scale) / IELTS: 6.5. overall / Duolingo: 105"`——同一所大学，CoE 收 Duolingo，Khoury 不收
- `✓ https://www.khoury.northeastern.edu/apply/masters-apply/` **外部学历评估不需要**：`"For international students, a WES evaluation is not required."`；学校自建 **Foreign Credential Evaluation (FCE)** 流程：`"At Northeastern, this is called a Foreign Credential Evaluation (FCE) process and often depends on comparability to a bachelor's degree received from a regionally accredited institution in the United States."` —— 与 UIUC/UT Dallas 的「不接受」不同，这里是「不需要」，下一步是「可以省这笔钱」

## 冻结点

- `✓ https://www.khoury.northeastern.edu/apply/masters-apply/` **固定截止日 + 滚动出结果并存**（本渠道池内唯一同时具备两者的样本）：`"Decision notification: Applications will be reviewed on a rolling basis as they are received."`——截止日管投递，滚动管出结果，两者不互斥
- `✓ 同上` **截止日按国际/国内 + 校区双维度分档，且明说会变**：`"Deadline is subject to change."`——具体日期是周期相关的动态值，不落盘到本文件，见 `programs.md` 的 `evidence` 列（本季尚未重新取证，`deadline` 仍 `待核实`）
